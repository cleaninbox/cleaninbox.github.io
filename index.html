How it works

Three steps to a cleaner inbox

No configuration, no complexity. Connect and clean in under a minute.

Step 1

Connect Gmail

Sign in with your Google account. We use OAuth 2.0 — we never see your password. Grant only the permissions shown.

Step 2

Scan & Categorize

We scan email metadata in real-time — subject lines, senders, sizes — and group them into smart categories. No email content is ever read.

Step 3

Review & Delete

Browse results, add items to your cart, and bulk-delete. Emails move to Trash first — nothing is permanently gone until you choose.

Features

Everything you need to declutter

Built to surface exactly what's wasting your storage, and remove it safely.

Smart categorization

Automatically identifies newsletters, promotions, social notifications, and large attachments using Gmail's own labels and header analysis.

Size-based cleanup

Sort by storage impact. The biggest emails rise to the top so you can reclaim gigabytes with just a few clicks instead of hundreds.

Safe deletion

Emails go to Trash first by default — giving you a 30-day recovery window. Permanent delete is opt-in only, always with a confirmation step.

Real-time progress

Watch categories populate live as the scan runs. Server-Sent Events stream results as they arrive — no polling, no waiting for a full report.

Privacy-first design

We only ever access email metadata — subject, sender, date, size, and labels. Your email body and attachment contents are never read or stored.

Bulk cart system

Add individual emails, entire categories, or custom-filtered results to a cart. Review before committing — then delete everything in one batch operation.

Privacy & Trust

We earn access, not assume it

Your inbox is personal. Here's exactly what we access, and exactly what we don't.

What we access

Email metadata only

Subject line, sender address, date, size, and Gmail labels — nothing more.

OAuth tokens, encrypted

Your access token is stored in an encrypted session cookie in your own browser — not on our servers.

Trash permission to delete

We request permission to move emails to Trash. Nothing is permanently deleted without your explicit confirmation.

What we never do

Never read email body content

The text of your emails and any attachments are completely off-limits.

Never sell or share your data

We don't share metadata with advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties.

Never lock you in

Revoke access any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Instant, permanent.
